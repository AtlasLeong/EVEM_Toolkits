# Market item icon provenance

These 48 icons were approved for the market page on 2026-09-27. They are direct
crops of user-provided EVE Echoes game screenshots, with no generative editing,
redrawing, background removal, or invented image content. The replacement mineral
screenshot is the authoritative source for the mineral group.

The approved set contains 1 currency, 7 fuels, 30 planetary resources, and 10
minerals. Original 144 × 124 pixel crops were proportionally resized and padded
to 128 × 128 pixels, then encoded as lossless WebP. The original opaque screenshot
background is retained. Item names, prices, and card borders are excluded.

Only the approved WebP exports are published here, renamed to `{itemId}.webp`
without changing their bytes. Catalog IDs identify assets independently of display
names; the application keeps the matching allowlist in `marketItemIcons.js`.
Unknown IDs or image-load failures use a decorative Lucide fallback.

Original screenshots, clipboard names, local paths, and crop-working files are
not distributed with the frontend. This provenance records the supplied source
and processing only; it does not grant or assert ownership of the game's artwork.
